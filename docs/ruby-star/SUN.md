# Sun challenge

[Overview](README.md) · [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md#modes-and-lobby)

`/challenge/sun` is a temperature fight against the Fiery King. Phase 2 adds the King's burning trail and Flare Spitters; phase 3 adds Scorch Sappers and sunbursts on rooms. Tuning is grouped in `constants.ts` as `SUN`; logic and state live in `sun.ts`, art in `sun-art.ts`, and the entry rule sheet in `SunGuide.tsx`.

On the first unlocked challenge entry, an arrival cinematic follows the intro's ship past the Sun's photosphere and onto the Solar Forge. The player faces the Fiery King, who grants the challenger a Ruby after the clear and keeps his own. Finishing the scene or skipping it with SKIP, Escape or Enter records the visit. Visual references: [flight](visual-reference/sun-arrival-flight.png), [docking approach](visual-reference/sun-arrival-forge.png), and [the guardian and gemstone](visual-reference/sun-arrival-guardian.png).

The Sun’s book button sits beside Begin Challenge on the star map and opens [The Last Shift](SUN-LORE.md), the story of the Solar Forge and its workers.

## Sun Explore prototype

The Last Shift map is inactive and retained in `game/SunExplore.tsx` and its supporting files. Its prototype flow plays the arrival, then opens the walking map. Move between floor tiles with the configured movement keys and press E to talk, read, or inspect. Sunbridge connects to the Royal Solar Forge, North Reservoir, Ward Four and the Ash Tunnel from the start, with service paths linking the districts. Mara or the blueprint introduces the Ruby socket test. Lyra, the tap or the speaking tube can confirm the heat reached Ward Four. Report to Mara to begin cooling, then ring the bridge bell to end the shift. The forge, workers and props reflect the ending. Milestones save in the desktop profile or browser storage.

Readable documents include short notes and a five-chapter account told by Mara and Pell. The chapters are spread across the districts and open one page at a time. The final chapter appears in Ward Four after the bell. The lunch counter, neighbors and other small stops are optional. The only bottom prompt appears when E can interact. The minimap follows the current walkable tiles, with structures and gaps cut out.

The 6,400 × 4,060 top-down world fills the game window. The camera stays centered at the rails, where lower service decks and the photosphere continue beyond the walkable area. Districts build on the battlefield plates with foundry grates, cyan coolant lanes, brass bridge paving, rose residential masonry, and soot in the Ash Tunnel. Props and NPCs sit on the same tile grid as the player. NPCs have idle suit movement and visor flickers. The player uses the original suit, and the King and statue share his battlefield model. Interaction names use Press Start 2P. ## Guardian and rage

- Three phases of 3000 HP (9000 total), shown in a three-segment guardian bar, phase 3 on the left. The phase follows lost HP and never reverses during normal play. Only the final defeat wins; there are no intermediate rewards.
- Rage starts at 0 and rises by 1.75 per second in phase 1 (`SUN.ragePerSecond`), 2.6 per second in phase 2 (`SUN.lateRagePerSecond`), and 2.73 per second in phase 3 (5% faster, `SUN.phase3RageMult`), capped at 100. A RAGE countdown-style bar under the guardian HP segments shows rage (the same row layout as other maps' bars); its amber color turns coral at 75%. His speed is .8 + .035 × rage pixels per tick. Water within eight tiles removes 20 rage, and every HP he loses removes 0.1 rage (`SUN.rageOnHit`).
- He retains his ordinary nearest-player/Ruby targeting and direct attacks. His existing arena wrath warns after ten seconds without HP damage, with a 6.5-second wind-up. Any HP damage interrupts it. If it lands, it also spawns 18 Cinder Wisps around the map.
- Every six seconds he spawns 1 + floor(rage / 25) wisps within four tiles of himself: one at no rage, five at full rage. Occupied or blocked tiles are skipped.
- Solar Flare (F) returns after a 27-second challenge cooldown. Here it deals exactly 4% of one phase (120, `SUN.solarShare`) to him and destroys all Cinder Wisps, including shielded ones. Other ordinary aliens use normal Solar damage and reward rules.
- Meteors deal 10% of one phase (300) to him and retain ordinary effects on the rest of the chamber. No minor space events occur.

## Temperature and Cinder Wisps

- Temperature ranges from 0 to 100 and starts at 50. A visible HUD bar with a blocky thermometer icon and warm edge light show it.
- Twelve wisps start scattered on open interior floor. Four more appear every four seconds on an independent timer, up to 80 living wisps total. They do not consume the ordinary alien spawn allowance. The separate No Wisps debug toggle suppresses them; No Mobs affects regular aliens only and leaves living wisps and every wisp spawn source alone.
- A Cinder Wisp has 3 HP, never moves or attacks, and adds .06 temperature per second while alive. Killing it stops that contribution without removing existing temperature. It gives no score, healing or energy and does not prevent manual Ruby healing.
- At 100 temperature a thermal eruption immediately deals 75 damage throughout the arena to the player, placed Ruby and all enemies except the King, then temperature returns to 50. Normal player/Ruby protection and shields apply; carried Ruby keeps its normal immunity. Alien defeats use meteor rewards. The eruption has a white-hot ignition flash, five thick expanding pressure fronts, outward molten streaks, falling sparks and edge flames, with camera shake and full-volume layered explosions. Damage and duration are unchanged by the cosmetic layers.

## Hydro Pulse (C)

- Eight-tile radius around the player's current position, 5.5-second cooldown (`waveCooldown`). A ready chime (`waveReady`) plays when the cooldown ends, but not between the rapid casts of the extinguished window. Clears every wisp in range, removes 2 temperature globally, deals 1 damage to other nearby targets, reduces nearby King's rage by 20, and extinguishes Emberlings. A C hit also reduces the King’s movement speed by 50% for 1.5 seconds (`waveSlowTicks`, `waveSlowMult`), refreshing on another hit without stacking; attacks retain their normal timing. In every phase, a C hit on the King also suppresses his extra rage growth and reduces the underlying growth by another 35% for ten seconds (`rageCalmTicks`, refreshed on another hit): 0.91 instead of 1.75 rage/second in phase 1, and 1.3 instead of 2.6 in phase 2 or 2.73 in phase 3 (`rageCalmMult = .8 × .65` in phase 1; `lateRageCalmMult = .65 / 1.3` later). The timer advances only during gameplay, including an extinguished window. Station ripples still calm existing rage but do not apply this C-only suppression. Cover does not block the ripple.
- It adds 45 percentage points to each cooling station in range, unless the King is within 2.5 tiles of that station. A station reaching full charge completes immediately.
- Water first extinguishes an Emberling or douses a Flare Spitter, then applies its damage without the flame armor.

## Cooling stations (hold Space)

- Five stations coexist on separated open floor tiles, with at least one in each of the four rooms. A room's last station relocates within that room when completed, preserving coverage; extra stations can relocate between rooms. If no eligible destination is available, the station stays in place. They appear as cyan reservoir tiles with charge bars and small cyan reservoir icons on the minimap.
- Hold Space within 1.6 tiles to charge the nearest station over five seconds and remove .6 temperature per second. An audible coolant-pump pulse plays while charge is being added, about three times per second, layered with the quiet Ruby-healing sound and the same player jiggle. Partial progress persists when released or when moving away. Near a station, Space takes priority over manual Ruby healing; elsewhere Space retains normal healing.
- At 100% charge the station heals the player 25 HP and Ruby 25 HP (`stationHealing`, `stationRubyHealing`, capped at maximum), sends out the same eight-tile ripple as normal Hydro Pulse, removes 11.96 temperature (`stationCooling`), resets to empty and relocates at least eight tiles away, avoiding other stations and occupied floor. Healing happens once per completion, whether charged with Space or completed by C. The station ripple does not charge other stations, so completions cannot chain indefinitely.
- When the King is within 2.5 tiles, progress resets to zero and charging is blocked. This is based on his current interpolated position. A red station and KING NEARBY cue show the interruption.
- Releasing Space, blur, hidden pages and dialogs clear held charging. Simulation, station progress, temperature, cooldowns and water effects pause with gameplay.

## Regular alien pool

Sun uses the [shared challenge alien pool](ENEMIES.md#shared-challenge-alien-pool), including Ghost, Bomber and Lancer after one minute and a rare Shooter. These selections occupy the remaining regular slots after the Emberling and Flare Spitter rolls.

## Emberlings

Regular aliens spawn at the normal rate (`SUN.alienSpawnRate = 1`), with the existing population cap; Cinder Wisp and Scorch Sapper timers remain independent. 40% of regular alien spawns become Emberlings. From phase 2, each regular spawn also has a 6% chance (`extraPlainChance`) to bring one extra ordinary alien from the shared pool, on top of the usual spawn and within the population cap; it replaces nothing and skips the Emberling and Spitter rolls. Among the remaining non-Emberling spawn slots, 35% become Flare Spitters from phase 2 while fewer than three are alive; at the cap those slots use the normal pool. Base HP 4 (ordinary alien scaling applies), speed 2.65, damage 9 to the player / 7 to Ruby, 70-tick attack cooldown. Melee hits knock you back two tiles. While burning, they take only 10% damage. Water permanently extinguishes them: movement and attacks stop, flame armor disappears, and they become a cold, fragile husk. Ordinary kill rewards apply. Extinguished Emberlings do not prevent manual healing.

At 90 heat and above, a strong red-orange map wash, scorched edge glow and travelling heat shimmer warn of eruption. The supplied extraterrestrial-alert recording sounds map-wide at original speed and pitch, repeating every .8 seconds, or every .4 seconds at 97 heat. Cooling below 90 resets it; zero heat silences it. It never schedules while paused.

## Flare Spitters

Available from phase 2, replacing 35% of non-Emberling regular spawn slots, capped at three living (`SUN.spitter`). They share ordinary spawn timing/population limits and respect No Mobs; Emberlings retain their 40% chance. Base HP 4 (like the Emberling) with ordinary alien scaling, with the same flame armor: it takes only 10% of damage (`SUN.spitter.armor`) until water douses it, speed 1.625, ten-tile attack range. A Spitter follows the player until within range and clear sight, stops and locks its aim, then warns for 42 ticks (0.7 seconds) before firing three fireballs at 0 and ±0.24 radians. Volley starts are 1.5 seconds apart (`attackCooldown`). Each fireball moves at 7.2px/tick, deals 6 damage to player or placed Ruby and stuns you for 0.8 seconds (`hitStun`; not Ruby), stops at cover and expires after about 1.4 seconds (83 ticks, the same maximum of about 12.5 tiles). When a fireball hits the player or Ruby, reaches its travel limit or hits a wall, its final floor tile burns for three seconds (`fireTicks`); wall impacts use the last floor tile before the wall. It uses the existing burning-ground damage/heat rules and water clears it. Ordinary kill rewards apply.

Hydro Pulse or station water cancels the current windup and douses it for good (`doused`): its flame armor is gone (the ripple damage already lands in full) and it is drawn in a cooled slate-blue shell from then on. There is no stun, and unlike an extinguished Emberling it keeps moving and spitting. Fireballs and their trails are drawn 50% larger, and hitting the player adds a brief flame-and-ember burst. The charcoal furnace model has bronze edges, two exhaust stacks and a wide orange mouth; the mouth swells and pipe flames rise during the warning, with three short amber aim marks. Fireballs are solid directional orange blocks with pale cores and short ember tails. The ordinary injured-only HP bar is shared. [Attack stages](visual-reference/sun-flare-spitter.png).

## Zero temperature

At zero temperature with a living King, he is extinguished and stunned for up to ten seconds: no movement, attacks, rage growth or new wisps. All other enemies move and attack at half speed throughout the zero-temperature window, including attack windups, cooldowns and Scorch Sapper actions (`SUN.quenchEnemyTickInterval = 2`). Normal rates return immediately when the window ends; player movement and attacks retain their normal rates. Temperature stays at zero during this window. His arena warning is cancelled and rage resets to zero.

The player gets a free speed burst (the normal 2s burst, with its sound) and five empowered Hydro Pulses with a .2-second cooldown between casts. Each keeps the normal local cooling/extinguishing effects, deals 10 damage to non-boss targets within eight tiles, and deals 4.5% of one phase (135) to the King if he is within eight tiles. The fifth cast retains its damage. After spending all five casts, the King remains stunned for a 2-second recovery delay (or until the 10-second window expires); then temperature returns to 50, the King resumes and C returns as an ordinary Hydro Pulse after a 2-second cooldown (`SUN.afterQuenchCooldown`, with its ready chime); using it starts the normal 6-second cooldown. Missed waves still spend a cast.

## God controls

Sun uses the same dark filled buttons, colored two-pixel borders and active phase highlighting as the other challenges. Wave Ready recharges C. Full Heat exits an extinguished window and sets temperature to 100, so the next tick runs the normal eruption. Zero Heat summons the King if absent, sets temperature to zero and grants a fresh full extinguished window with five empowered casts. Heat Up and Cool Down adjust temperature by 10 points per click, clamped to 0–100; reaching an endpoint uses the same extinguish/eruption behavior as Zero Heat/Full Heat. Heating above zero exits the extinguished window and restores the normal C cooldown. All heat buttons require playing state. Phase 1/2/3 controls retain their existing behavior. No Wisps clears living Cinder Wisps without rewards or temperature loss and blocks natural, King-proximity and arena-attack wisp spawns. It leaves regular aliens alone, works independently of No Mobs, and persists through restart. Disabling it resumes the independent wisp timers.

## Phase 2: burning trail

- From phase 2, while rage is at least 20 (`SUN.trailRage`), each floor tile the King steps onto ignites a centered 3×3 patch (`SUN.trailRadius = 1`) for 10s, excluding walls and teleport pads (`trailTicks`, refreshed if he steps there again; at most 80 burning tiles, oldest dropped first). Below 20 rage, or in phase 1, he leaves none.
- Every 0.5s (`trailGap`), standing on a burning tile costs 3 HP (`trailDamage`) and adds 0.5 heat; a placed Ruby on one takes 2. Normal protection and shields apply.
- Water puts it out: every Hydro Pulse ripple (including station bursts and empowered waves) clears burning tiles within its eight-tile radius, and reaching zero heat puts out the whole trail.
- **Look:** a dark ember plate with a pulsing orange inset, three stepped tapering flames (wide base, narrower body, pale tip) that flicker and sway, and an orange rim; it fades over its last second. The minimap shows burning tiles in orange. `drawBurningTile` in `sun-art.ts` draws it for both the battlefield and the rule sheet.

## Phase 3: Scorch Sappers

- From phase 3, a Scorch Sapper (`scorch_sapper`, 10 HP, speed 2.9) arrives every 8s (`SUN.sapperGap`) on open floor at least 6 tiles from you, with at most 2 alive (`sapperCap`). They never count toward the regular alien cap and never stop manual Ruby healing.
- It ignores you and Ruby and never attacks. It walks to the nearest cooling station; once beside it, it drains 12% of the station's charge per second (`sapperDrain`) and adds 0.5 heat per second (`sapperHeat`, not while the King is extinguished). A spark tether links it to the station, and that station blinks orange on the minimap.
- Hydro Pulse deals ordinary damage instead of destroying it instantly. Normal alien defeat rewards apply.
- **Look:** deliberately not a flame: a dark armoured siphon beetle with a bronze outline (so it reads on the dark floor), a basalt shell with thin molten seams, two small ember eyes, six stepping legs, a brass siphon nozzle on the side it faces (towards the station while draining), and a glass tank of stolen cyan coolant on its back that sloshes, fills and glows while draining, with small steam puffs. Its drain tether is a cyan coolant line.

An attached Sapper blocks manual and Hydro Pulse station charging while draining. It removes 12 charge points/second and adds 0.5 heat/second; existing charge drains gradually rather than resetting. The station shows no label: the drain reads through its blinking orange charge and the spark line. Removing or interrupting the Sapper restores charging. While actually draining nonzero charge it plays a quiet `celestial/empty-charge.wav` cue at most once per second. Water deals normal damage; it is not an instant kill.

## Phase 3: sunbursts

- From phase 3, every 10–14s (`SUN.flareGap`) one random room is marked: it gains persistent gold corner brackets and a dark single-line SUNBURST countdown panel in its upper third. The minimap shows matching gold corner brackets and a compact icon-only sunburst badge, without countdown text, including when a meteor marks the same room. A SUNBURST: ROOM banner also shows. The extraterrestrial alert repeats across the whole map at a raised pitch (1.2 times playback rate), every .8 seconds initially and every .4 seconds in the final two seconds; it stops when the warning ends. The King's fire spell accents the initial marking. After 8s (`flareWarning`) it flares: the arena gains 25 heat (`flareHeat`, which can trigger the eruption), you and a placed Ruby take 25 damage if inside that room (`flareDamage`; normal protection applies), and it shows a white-hot room flash, expanding layered fire fronts, rising blocky flame columns and outward ember fragments, confined to the marked room, with a short shake. The Solar Flare cue and King fire spell layer with full-volume sci-fi and bomb explosions. The countdown uses the same single-line Press Start 2P font treatment as the meteor warning, with the name followed by seconds. Gold framing distinguishes Sunburst from the orange meteor warning; there is no emblem or depletion bar.
- **Defusing:** any Hydro Pulse ripple landing in that room (including a station burst there), or casting Solar Flare from anywhere, stops it and cools the arena by 5 (`flareDefuseCool`), with a cyan room flash.
- No flares while the King is extinguished; a pending one is cancelled then. The burning trail from phase 2 continues.

## Rule sheet

The Sun sheet opens with bold warm-gold guidance to control temperature, fill cooling stations and keep the King's rage down. Cards are grouped under General Rules & Skills (temperature, stations, Hydro Pulse, extinguishing and Solar Flare), Enemies (King, rage, wisps and Emberlings), Phase 2 (burning trail and Flare Spitter) and Phase 3 (Sunburst and Scorch Sapper). Descriptions emphasize actions, consequences and counters, including slowing enemies at zero heat and stopping Sunbursts with Solar Flare from anywhere. Station instructions show the active heal binding.

Each challenge has an animated CELESTIAL SKILL (PRESS F) card alongside its SPECIAL SKILL (PRESS C) card. Sun describes Solar Flare, Neptune describes Neptune’s Tide, Gaia describes Gaia’s Roots (including clearing vines and preventing regrowth), and Moon describes Lunar Echo and the real Queen’s immunity to its bait. These cards reuse the actual celestial cast renderer.

## Look and checks

An obsidian solar forge suspended over the Sun's photosphere. The docking and guardian shots share a stepped forecourt with open corners, flanking platforms, a broad central floor, and an inset ship berth. The forecourt uses the map's heat-shield plates, brass-capped cover, and animated gameplay backdrop, and the Fiery King uses his combat model.

- **Backdrop:** near-black plum and a soft ember horizon, three broad drifting plasma currents with thin warm cores and a few travelling glints, the vast limb of the star rising at the lower left: a dark molten disc brightening to a blazing rim under flickering corona rays, with faint irregular convection cells and a sunspot pair. A giant prominence grows from the rim over about 15 seconds at 60 ticks/s, erupts with a flare (footpoint bursts and a brief warm sky wash) and flings a broken shell of plasma outward, then reforms; solar-wind streaks race away from the star. Two small prominence loops and one large faint arc carry travelling plasma beads, plus five widely spaced stepped magnetic plumes in a deeper parallax layer, broad solar-cloud silhouettes and sparse ember clusters. The large currents and distant silhouettes provide visible detail while retaining open dark space; keep convection cells irregular and faint, without outlines, and avoid overlapping bright arches. The world itself is the Sun; its own limb appears, but there is no separate sun in its sky.
- **Chamber colors:** ALPHA gold `#ffd35c`, BETA coral `#ff727e`, DELTA orange `#ff9a45`, GAMMA dawn rose `#eeb1d6`. The same palette drives borders, teleport pads, floor inlays, minimap and destination picker.
- **Floor:** dark plum/charcoal heat-shield plates, sparse brass rivets and inlaid seals, and joined molten seams that slowly carry light. The seams brighten with temperature and are decorative, not damage tiles. Engraved sun-wheel lines surround each teleport pad; pads keep their original gameplay footprint and destination colors.
- **Cover:** solid raised basalt blocks with joined brass caps, dark recessed faces and either a carved solar seal or a narrow caged molten core. Exposed rims remain clear; the art uses the existing collision map.
- **Cooling stations:** glass cyan reservoirs on four brass clamps, with an animated waterline rising with charge, small bubbles, glass reflections and a segmented progress strip. The KING NEARBY blocked warning remains. Cyan minimap reservoir icons show the fill level; blocked icons turn coral and drained stations blink orange. There is no HOLD SPACE label above stations. Blocked reservoirs turn warm red.
- **Temperature:** heat strengthens the glowing seams, horizon haze and distant prominence light. A restrained edge vignette replaces the flat orange arena wash, keeping actors readable. Reaching zero starts a 75-tick arena-wide cyan-white extinguishing flash, expanding pressure fronts, frost streaks and a pulse over every minimap chamber, with a 12-tick impact shake at strength 6. During the extinguished window the King retains his armor, shoulders, crown and four eyes in pale brown/bone colors, with no flames, glow, orbiting accents or particles. His old fire-shot trail and wrath pulse clear immediately; a small remaining-cast label stays above him. Eruption uses a bright initial flash and three expanding square fronts.
- **Hydro Pulse HUD:** a stepped cyan droplet above two angular ripple bands, inside the same 42px frame as the celestial and Moon Mark icons. A small C hint identifies the key. Cooldown dims the glyph and fills the perimeter; empowered mode turns it pale and shows five small remaining-cast pips. No text-only skill tile.
- **Units and water:** wisps retain their pale flame core and ember fragments. Emberlings use a larger, heavy armored silhouette (body fraction 0.72) and become slate-blue husks when extinguished. Water has expanding angular rings and droplets, with pale empowered casts. Empowered waves add a fast cyan-white pressure front, three trailing rings, larger directional spray, a brief central geyser and a compact impact flare; their radius, damage and 36-tick lifetime stay the same. Each empowered cast adds an 18-tick camera shake at strength 10, a short icy viewport flash, a larger geyser with eight water jets, and a layered bass/pressure impact sound.

Sun scenery uses `gameTick` so its motion pauses with the simulation. Rendering is deterministic and does not mutate game state.
