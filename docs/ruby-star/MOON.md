# Moon challenge

[Overview](README.md) · [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md#modes-and-lobby)

Regular alien spawns use the [shared challenge pool](ENEMIES.md#shared-challenge-alien-pool): Ghost, Bomber and Lancer join after 60 seconds, with Shooter rare. The map’s themed enemies keep their own replacement chances and phase rules.

`/challenge/moon`: an illusion fight against the Shadow Queen. Neptune is about finding things and Gaia about holding ground; the Moon is about reading which Queen is real. Everything shared by challenges (stage 0, guardian-only boss, recharging celestial skill, win on defeat) is in [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md). Values live in `constants.ts` as `MOON_*`. Logic is `moon.ts` (`state.moon`, with short hooks in `engine.ts`), art is `moon-art.ts` (the theme switches on via `isMoonMap`), and the rule sheet is `MoonGuide.tsx`.

**Three phases:** phase 2 adds mirrors and mimics, phase 3 adds darkness. Defeating phase 3 wins.

The Moon’s book button beside Begin Challenge opens [One Last Dance](MOON-LORE.md), a tale about the Shadow Queen, Nella and the town’s memory mirrors.

## The fight

- **HP:** three phases of 1850 (5550 total) on a three-segment guardian bar (phase 3 left), with the name line SHADOW QUEEN · PHASE n. The bar is not live while she is disguised: it shows her HP as of her last reveal or jump (`shownHp`) and catches up while she is revealed and when she jumps, so a hit on a disguised target never shows at once. Her phase follows that shown HP and never goes back; each new phase shows a PHASE n banner. God panel: Phase 1/2/3 buttons jump to that phase at full phase HP, summoning her first if needed, and Mark Ready recharges the Moon Mark at once. Her own hits deal 0.4× of the usual Queen damage (about 2.8 to you, 4 to Ruby). Her jumps never heal her.
- **Arrival:** she first appears with her teleport sound and the same arrival frame as her jumps. The purple TELEPORT bar below her HP counts down to her next jump and refills when she lands.
- **Disguise:** she wears her Echo's look (model, flicker, bolt colour and minimap marker), so she looks exactly like her illusions.
- **Echo minimap marker:** every unit with the Echo look (the disguised Queen, her illusions and mimics while posing) shows on the minimap as a hollow magenta frame (8px on desktop: `#ff7ae6` rim, dark inset, pale core), drawn over ordinary alien dots so it stands out from mites, shades and walking mimics. Endless Queen echoes use the same marker.
- **Illusions** (`moon_illusion`, 4 HP):
  - Her first jump brings 4 and every jump after brings 2, appearing the same instant she lands: one in her new chamber and the rest in chambers other than yours.
  - At most 6 are alive in phase 1 and 8 from phase 2 (`MOON_CLONE_CAPS`); past that the oldest fades out.
  - They are stationary and run her exact AI (target choice, range, 40-tick windup, 70-tick cooldown), shooting for 3 to you and 2 to Ruby.
  - They give no score, energy or kill rewards. Her jump no longer leaves permanent Echoes.
- **Reveal:** for the last 1.5s (90 ticks) before each jump, she shows her true model with a breaking-mask burst and sound (see [Audio](AUDIO.md#moon-reveal)), and takes 2.5× damage. A screen-space crystal overlay (`drawMoonRevealOverlay`) accompanies it: a brief pale flash, then large flat translucent crystal silhouettes with pale contours grow in from the corners and edges with violet frost and glints, peaking just after the mask breaks and melting away before she jumps. It is cosmetic only.
- **Shatter:** an illusion destroyed by anything other than a meteor bursts into a ring of 8 crystal shards. They fly about 9 tiles at 5px/tick and stop at walls. A shard deals 6 to you or 4 to a placed Ruby, once.
- **Delayed heal:** 31.5% (`MOON_HIT_HEAL`) of the HP she loses to your attacks is banked (`healBank`) and heals you by that amount when she next jumps, and Ruby by the same amount if it is with you (carried, or placed in your room). It never heals on the hit, so damage alone does not give her away right away. Illusions, meteors and other sources earn nothing. When a banked heal is paid out, her departure chamber gets a 1.5-second violet/ice-blue floor pulse, an opening broken-square seal, a broad soft lunar aura and denser rising crystal glints. Open square auras, orbiting solid crystal fragments and rising healing crosses surround the player and Ruby when it shares the heal. The `gem_passive_2.wav` recording plays once with the healing pulse; see [Audio](AUDIO.md#moon-challenge). The effect captures her old location before teleporting, stays clipped to the chamber’s open floor, and pauses with gameplay. [Healing pulse reference](visual-reference/moon-healing.png).
- **Lunar Echo:** her illusions are drawn to your decoy like any enemy, but the real Queen ignores it and keeps shooting you, so a decoy can give her away. In code, the decoy-substituted enemy pass skips her and a second pass updates her against the real player.
- **Tell:** only the real Queen casts a reflection on the crystal floor: a faint violet glow and a mirrored tiara below her.
- **Moon Mark (C):** marks the nearest Queen or illusion for 6s (blinking in the last second), then recharges 10s from the cast. A HUD icon right of the celestial skill icon shows it, in the same style: ice-blue sigil glowing with a C hint when ready, dimmed with a filling perimeter while recharging. The mark rides on whoever wears it: a block-built ice-blue moon shining down from above, four solid corner brackets with pale lips, three gently expanding translucent corner echoes, four orbiting ice-blue crystals and twelve twinkling white-blue sparkles, plus an ice-blue frame on the minimap. Casting releases a 32-tick expanding ice-blue octagonal wave from the player’s cast position, with a very faint pale leading core and a highly transparent blue body and short wake, eight translucent ice-blue crystal tips and 48 faint translucent travelling glitter glints with softly twinkling white cores reaching about 3.2 tiles; it is cosmetic. It also opens a small square seal at the player, sends three rectangular lights toward the recipient and expands a broken square impact seal as the brackets settle into place. A ice-blue moon hovers above and to the side of the target, casting a feathered blue spotlight down onto it. Two overlapping stepped translucent halos surround its symmetric block-built body, exactly 40px wide and 40px high, smoothly pulsing between 70% and 42% opacity over a two-second cycle before its arrival/expiry fade; flat blue crater clusters, pale deposits and detached glints enrich the surface without bevels. Eighteen subtle crystal motes drift down through the beam. The moon follows the marked body and fades in and out smoothly; its light renders behind enemies so their models stay clear. There is no full-tile wash. Cast animation derives from remaining mark ticks and freezes with gameplay; the captured cast origin is cosmetic. If it was her, it follows her to her next chamber; an illusion's mark stays put when she jumps. It reveals nothing by itself.
- **Mirror mites** (`mirror_mite`, speed 1.1, melee 4 to you / 3 to Ruby) are about 42% of regular alien spawns (60% of what is left after the shade and hound rolls). They always spawn with 1 HP and any damaging hit kills one, even a weak bullet (the Spook rule in `damageEnemy`), and a kill heals you +10 on top of the usual +5. A kill also lights two rooms violet on the minimap for 5s, fading in the last second, or until she jumps: the room she is in and one other, in random order. They are the fight's healing source, offsetting her already-high base damage.
- **Shades (phase 2+)** (`moon_shade`, 11 HP, speed 2, cannot be knocked back, melee 6 to Ruby / 4 to you, every 1.2s) are Ruby hunters that stay in the chamber they spawned in (`updateMoonShade`, `Enemy.shade.home`): they attack a placed Ruby only while it is in that chamber, and you only while you carry Ruby there (or a Lunar Echo decoy there); otherwise they wander their chamber. Their paths are searched with everything outside the chamber treated as wall, so they never cut through a hallway. A shade walks directly to Ruby, strikes from whichever side it reaches and waits there between strikes. Each hit on a placed Ruby also shoves it 3 tiles, one tile every 5 ticks: straight away from the shade, or in the next best open direction when that is blocked or would bring Ruby onto or beside the chamber's teleport pad (`rubyShoveDir`) (`MOON_SHADE_RUBY_PUSH`, `MOON_RUBY_PUSH_STEP`, `moonShadePushRuby`); walls and cover stop it, and picking Ruby up cancels the shove. Every shove costs the shade 30% of its max HP (`MOON_SHADE_PUSH_COST`), so the fourth kills it; a shade that dies this way gives no star energy. From phase 2, spawns roll for a shade first: 16% of regular spawns are shades.
- **Crystal Hounds** (`moon_hound`, 10 HP, speed 3, melee 4, 75-tick attack cooldown) are fast melee chasers, like Gaia's Ragecap, that only ever hunt you, never Ruby: each bite on you also knocks you back 2 tiles with the normal knockback (`MOON_HOUND.knockback`). They roll right after shades, 16% of what is left (about 13% of regular spawns), at most 4 alive (`MOON_HOUND`), in every phase.
- **Mimics** (`moon_mimic`, 5 HP, speed 0.9, harmless): from phase 1, while the Queen is present, one arrives every 6s on its own timer (`MOON_MIMIC_GAP`, at most 4 alive) on a free wall-side spot at least 5 tiles from you. Mimics never replace other spawns or count toward the regular alien cap. It never chases or attacks. In its own form it wanders its room for 2–4s; then it walks to the nearest free, reachable wall-side spawn spot (where she and her illusions stand), picking the next nearest if that one gets taken, and only disguises once it stands there. Once disguised it stays that way until destroyed, drawn exactly like an illusion (model, flicker, minimap dot) and counted by the Moon Mark. Disguised, it has only 1 HP (one laser or three bullets), stands still, cannot be pushed and never attacks, which is the tell: an illusion that never shoots. Killed, it gives normal alien rewards and no shards. State is `Enemy.mimic`; `updateMoonMimic` skips the normal AI while disguised.
- **Mirrors (phase 2+):** when phase 2 starts, every room gets 5 straight 3-tile strips of mirror floor (`state.moon.mirrors`) on open floor away from pads, kept apart from each other and never under you. A strip is never placed right alongside a wall: one with a wall or cover block beside two or more of its tiles on the same side is rejected, since that wall already stops your shots; a strip one tile away from a wall is fine. Every 20s (`MOON_MIRROR_SHIFT`) they all move to new spots, the old panes fading out as the new ones fade in with a pale flash over 0.5s. They stay through phase 3. You can walk over them, but your laser (which fires in all four directions) and bullets stop at a mirror and bounce 80% of their damage (`MOON_MIRROR_REFLECT`) back into you (about 1.81 laser / 0.28 bullet, more when powered); nothing behind the mirror is hit. Waves, bombs and skills pass. The minimap shows them as faint silver marks.
- **Blinkers (phase 3)** (`blinker`, the Endless alien): from phase 3, 22% of regular alien spawns are Blinkers (`MOON_BLINKER_CHANCE`, rolled before shades), at most three alive (`MOON_BLINKER_MAX`). As in Endless, killing one teleports you to a random free floor tile in any chamber.
- **Darkness (phase 3):** darkness moves to previously lit rooms every 10s (`MOON_DARK_SHIFT`) with a 0.5s cross-fade. Each placement has a 35% chance to darken two rooms (`MOON_DOUBLE_DARK_CHANCE`). Dark chambers show a dim stepped eclipse on the floor and shimmer with dense, small plum, indigo and violet floor glints. A stronger translucent violet screen wash with drifting shadow bands and silver motes eases in and out over 0.4 seconds as you enter or leave darkness. Walls, cover, the room outline, the teleport pad, the player, Ruby, bombs, Moon Mark and attack effects remain visible. Darkness hides enemies, pickups, mirrors and normal floor detail. The minimap shows the dark room as a black wash. Anything hit inside shows itself for 0.75s (`MOON_SEEN_TICKS`). While she is revealed, the Queen still shows on the minimap even inside the dark.
- **Meteors** deal 10% of one phase (200) to her and wipe illusions in that room without shards.
- **No space events.** Her defeat clears every illusion and shard.

## Rule sheet

The rule sheet has fourteen animated cards grouped under General Rules & Skills, Enemies (Shadow Queen, her hiding, reveal and shattering illusions, then mirror mite, mimic and Crystal Hound), Phase 2+ (mirrors and shades) and Phase 3 (darkness and Blinkers). A bold warm-gold introduction describes the Queen's disguise, Moon Mark and Lunar Echo. Each card briefly states what its subject does, without instructions or tactical advice.

## Look

A shadowy crystal cavern under the lunar surface: crystalline, magical and mysterious. There is no moon in the sky, since you are already on it.

- **Room colours:** amethyst, violet, orchid and ice blue (`MOON_CHAMBER_COLORS`), used on the battlefield borders, minimap and teleport picker. Ice blue `#a9e6ff` is the magic accent throughout.
- **Cover blocks:** every block is solid crystal (`drawMoonCrystalBlock`). Five broad cuts mix diagonal splits, off-centre facets, upright cleavage faces and stepped tabular crystals. Each is lit from the top left, mostly in the room colour with occasional orchid or cool-blue material. Sparse smoky stepped inclusions sit inside the crystal rather than adding surface ornaments. Some tiles hold a deeper inner facet that pulses with an ice-blue spark. Thin pale facet edges, a bright rim only where a block meets open floor, and a pale glint that sweeps diagonally across all crystal at once.
- **Floor:** near-black polished crystal. Two soft diagonal sheens sweep across it (a broad violet one and a thin ice-blue one), faint grain lines brighten as the sheen passes, and each crystal block reflects a fading glow into the floor tile below it. Large dark mist patches drift slowly over the floor. A few tiles hold a small set gem that twinkles, and rare runes (a square frame, a turning diamond, four corner marks) fade in and out. Pads stay clean.
- **Mirror mite:** a small blocky crystal bug: a pale lilac rim, a dark mirror face with a lit facet and a glint sweeping across it, two pale eyes, a stepped mirror shard on its back (blocks, pale on the lit side and violet on the other) and three pairs of bent legs that scuttle.
- **Crystal Hound:** a wide ice-blue block with a glowing rim and dark face, two pale stepped shard ears (wide at the base, narrowing to a square tip), narrowed pale eyes under angry ice brows that slant in, two fangs that drop as it bites, and four galloping legs.
- **Mimic:** in its own form, a soft lavender-grey shapeshifter cube whose four horizontal slices keep sliding out of line, one large eye with an ice-blue glint, and a faint flicker of the Queen's tiara above it. Disguised, it is drawn exactly as an illusion.
- **Mirror tile:** a see-through framed silver-lilac pane (the floor shows beneath, so it reads as walkable) with a bright glint sliding across it. A bounced shot shows a pale streak from the mirror back to you and a small cross flash on the pane.
- **Shade:** a translucent hooded wraith in deep violet that flickers between see-through and nearly solid, with a lit left edge, a dark face, two ice-blue eyes that flare (and claws that come out) as it strikes, and a ragged tail of drifting blocks.
- **Motes:** violet and ice-blue motes with short tails rise through the rooms on fixed world columns.
- **Backdrop:** the dark of a vast geode. Stepped crystal spires rise from below and hang from above in two parallax layers; the near ones have glowing tips and pulsing veins, and drips gather and fall from the hanging tips. Behind them, stepped far cavern walls with faint crystal seams close in at the top and bottom, and small glowing geode clusters dot the dark. Faint prism shafts sway, deep violet mist drifts, lone crystal shards turn slowly, crystal dust twinkles, and a shadow vignette closes in at the top and bottom.
