# Ruby Star TL;DR

[Overview](README.md)

**Core loop.** You're a square ship protecting Ruby (a pink gem) across four rooms in a 2×2 grid: ALPHA top-left, BETA top-right, GAMMA bottom-right, DELTA bottom-left. If either of you hits 0 HP, the run ends. Tools: laser, held-charge wave, bullets (every 20th is heavy), speed burst, bomb, carrying Ruby, healing next to her, and teleport pads. Filling star energy makes your next ability powered. Hazards: meteors move clockwise through the rooms, comets start at stage 3, and four rare space events: Black Hole, Shooting Star, Eclipse and Supernova.

**Endless Space.** Stage = bosses defeated, with 10 boss encounters. Alien types and difficulty tiers unlock over time. Every 3 boss kills you pick one of 3 constellation upgrades (44 total). From stage 10 (Ascension), boss kills give a one-use celestial skill on F, and each skill changes your suit. From stage 20 (Transcendence), two bosses can be active at once and you choose a gemstone blessing every 10 stages. Shooter, Reflector and Trapper join at stage 10; Enchanter, Bleeder, Snatcher and Impostor at stage 20. Ascension mode starts at stage 10.

**Celestial Challenge.** A star map of 10 worlds. Sun, Neptune, Gaia, Moon, Jupiter, Saturn, Binary Star, Sirius, Venus and the Singularity are playable. Each world is a three-phase fight against its guardian with its own aliens, a primal skill on C (middle-click), a recharging F skill, a world look and an animated picture-card rule sheet:

- **Sun:** temperature, cooling stations, Cinder Wisps.
- **Neptune:** dives and clones, fish, the Trident.
- **Gaia:** overgrowth territory, Sunfire, Sunstones.
- **Moon:** illusions, reveal, mirrors, darkness.
- **Jupiter:** charge, storm pursuit, Shard Race, Volt Fence.
- **Saturn:** break or take the frost shields she gives her aliens, and time Absolute Zero (a time-stop field) against her grants and recalls.

**Visual style.** "Blocky neon-vector": crisp square shapes with a glowing rim, dark inset and pale core, with only light glow. Moving parts rotate as whole shapes, never as pixel staircases. Each alien, boss and celestial effect keeps its own look.

**Rule sheets and wording.** Short, plain lines with no numbers or technical detail. Key names come from the player's current bindings. Alien entries are one sentence plus a "Killing buff: …" line; boss entries end with "Celestial skill: NAME". Detail lives in the topic docs, which are kept current with every change.
