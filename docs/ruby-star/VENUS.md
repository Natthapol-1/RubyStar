# Venus

[Overview](README.md) · [World visuals](MAP-PREVIEWS.md#venus-the-fallen-citadel)

Regular alien spawns use the [shared challenge pool](ENEMIES.md#shared-challenge-alien-pool): Ghost, Bomber and Lancer join after 60 seconds, with Shooter rare. The map’s themed enemies keep their own replacement chances and phase rules.

`/challenge/venus` is a three-phase fight against the Aegis Sentinel in the Fallen Citadel, built around memory: **remember where each totem was buried and the order they appeared in, and break them in that order.** Tuning is `VENUS` in `venus.ts`; rules are `venus.ts` with hooks in `engine.ts`; art is `venus-art.ts` (scenery stays in `world-themes.ts`); the rule sheet is `VenusGuide.tsx`; the C icon is `SandSweepStatus.tsx`.

Regular aliens spawn as in the other challenges, meteors fall, and space events are off. A meteor always hits him for 10% of a phase, 100 (`meteorShare`; 110 in phase 3), whether his shield is up or not.

Venus’s book button beside Begin Challenge opens [Beyond the Command](VENUS-LORE.md), the tale of the Aegis Sentinel and the people of the Citadel.

## The shield and the totem sequence

- **Phases:** one HP pool of three bands, 1000 / 1000 / 1100 HP (`phaseHP`, `finalPhaseHP`; 3100 in all); every Venus reflection (Sand Swirl guard, Venus Veil, a reflected totem blast) deals 85% of the damage (`reflectMult`; Venus Veil has no boss cap here), and onto him it is also scaled by `phaseHP` / 400 (`reflectBaseHP`), so it stays the same share of his HP; one hit never crosses a band. Each phase starts shielded with a totem sequence of 4 / 5 / 5 totems (`totems`); phase 3 adds his hidden core instead of another totem.
- **Shielded:** as everywhere, he takes 5% damage and reflects each hit back at you.
- **The sequence:** totems (`aegis_construct` bodies with `venusOrder`) appear one at a time every 2 seconds (`spawnGap`) on random open floor anywhere, at least 4 tiles from you, spread out: at most 2 in one chamber (`totemsPerRoom`) and at least 7 tiles apart (`totemSpacing`), relaxed only when nothing fits. Each shows itself and its number for 3 seconds (`revealTicks`), then the sand buries it: it leaves `state.enemies`, so it is off the map and minimap and nothing can hit it. Each has 60 HP (`totemHP`).
- **In order:** a totem's number shows on the minimap only during its first appearance (its 3 second reveal), never in the world or later, so you track their order yourself. Any totem can be damaged, but breaking one out of order blows it up at once like an overload (its whole chamber is hit) and it reappears elsewhere at full HP with the same number. Breaking one in order heals you and Ruby 16 (`totemHeal`) and hits him for 1% of a phase (10 HP; 11 in phase 3, `totemBossShare`), even through his shield.
- **Out of time.** Newly spawned totems start their first overload after 31 / 27 / 23 seconds in phases 1 / 2 / 3. After a totem moves, its usual 30 / 26 / 22 second timer applies.
- **Overload:** as in endless, the totem shows itself and glows amber for 1.5 seconds (`overloadTicks`), then blows: everyone in its chamber takes 60 damage (`blastDamage`; endless keeps 80). It reappears elsewhere, revealed, at full HP and with the same number; the sequence's progress is unchanged. If you are in that chamber with the Sand Swirl guard up or Venus Veil active, the blast is reflected: 85% of it, about 51, hits every enemy in the chamber (him scaled to about 128 with his HP, through his shield; totems, relics and his core are spared), shown as gold lines, and as reflection damage it never bounces back to you.
- **Misses heal him:** every totem that blows and moves, whether it overloaded or was broken out of order, heals him 4% of a phase (40; 44 in phase 3, `missHeal`), never past his current phase, with his heal flash.
- **Shield broken:** destroying the last totem breaks the shield for 12 seconds (`exposeTicks`), when he takes full damage, and heals you and Ruby 30 (`shieldHeal`). The backlash also deals him 18% of a phase, 180 (`breakShare`; only 8%, 88, in the final phase, `finalBreakShare`; never past his current phase), drawn across the whole screen wherever you are (centred on him, held inside the screen when he is off it): a cyan-white flash, his shield dome bursting, thick cracks and three bold diamond shockwaves racing out from him, a storm of big outlined shield shards that spin and fall, glitter, a cyan frame and a hard screen shake (about 1.7 seconds, `shatterTicks`). Then the shield returns with a fresh sequence. Reaching a new phase also brings the shield back with that phase's sequence. After the shield recovers, the first totem appears about 2.5 seconds later (`recoverySpawnDelay`).

## Phase 3: the hidden core

Every phase 3 sequence also buries his core (`sand_core`, 1 HP so any hit breaks it, `coreHP`) on a random open tile away from you, the totems, relics and nests, with no hint where it is. A Sand Swirl whose reach covers it uncovers it for 5 seconds like a totem (`sweepExpose`), then the sand takes it again. While buried, the core appears in a dashed cyan outline and as a minimap beacon with Seer's Eye. The God panel shows its model on the floor and a miniature on the minimap.

The core is sealed while any totem of the sequence is left: hits do nothing and play a lock sound. Each totem destroyed in order leaves a blocky, see-through cyan arrow with a thick shaft and an outer glow on its tile pointing toward the core (in one of eight directions) for 4 seconds (`clueTicks`). Destroying the last totem no longer breaks the shield; it opens the core and starts a 15 second window (`coreTicks`, a cyan CORE bar in the HUD). Destroying the core in time breaks the shield as usual. If the window runs out, the shield recovers: a fresh sequence starts with a new buried core. As a hint, once only one totem is left, his core's chamber pulses cyan on the minimap. Once the core window opens, that cyan square shrinks steadily over the window (`venusCoreHintRect`), ending as a 9×9-tile square that holds the core at a random spot inside it (`coreHintOff`). The same area shows on the floor in the world: a faint cyan wash with a thick, pulsing dashed cyan border that closes in with it.

It is drawn as a small cube in his style built around a big glowing cyan core: a dark rim, thin gold corner brackets and a dark aperture holding the core. Sealed, the core glows a steady soft cyan; open, the brackets part and it beats bright with a strong glow, a pale heart and a pulsing cyan outline. On the minimap it is a small diamond (gold-ringed while sealed) while uncovered.

## Primal skill: Sand Swirl (C)

Clears the sand in a blocky octagon of 37 tiles: a 7×7 square with three tiles cut from each corner, so each outer edge is 3 tiles long, centred on the tile you occupy at cast time (3 tiles out each way, `sweepRadius`): every buried totem there shows itself for 5 seconds (`sweepExpose`). Recharges in 1.25 seconds (`sweepCooldown`).

**Guard:** for 0.75 seconds after a sweep (`guardTicks`), any direct hit on you deals only 5% of its damage (`guardDamage`) and 85% of its damage is reflected to the attacker (`guardReflect` × `reflectMult`), shown as a gold line. It works on top of Venus Veil: each cuts and reflects separately. Reflection into the shielded Sentinel is cut by his shield like any other damage. The guard also catches a totem blast: it blocks all of it, you heal 25 (`counterHeal`) and send out the counter shockwave, with nothing reflected to the totem.

**Parry:** a Sand Swirl used within 20 ticks (about 0.33 seconds) before his leap landing or lance hits you (`parryWindow`) turns it back on him (a leap can be parried from up to a tile outside its landing reach, `leapParryReach`, even though it would not have hit you there): you take nothing, heal 25 (`counterHeal`), send out the counter shockwave and he is stunned for 2 seconds (`parryStun`), or 3.5 seconds for a parried leap (`leapParryStun`). He loses 6% of a phase's HP (`parryDamage`, never past a phase) only while his shield is broken; through the shield he takes the usual 5% of that.

**Counter shockwave:** every successful counter (a parry or a guarded totem blast) makes Sand Swirl ready again at once, with its ready chime, and hits every other enemy within 3.5 tiles of you for 25 (`counterBlastRadius`, `counterBlastDamage`), matching its diamond shockwaves. It hits visible totems too (25 of their 60 HP; breaking one this way counts like any break, so an out-of-order one still blows and moves). It never hits him (he takes only the parry's own damage), relics or his core, and buried units are untouched.

## Sand Scarab nests

Each totem spawns with a buried nest on a random tile within three tiles of it (`nestRange`), and every 3 seconds (`nestGap`) another nest is buried near the totem with the fewest (ties at random, so they spread evenly), up to 8 / 10 / 10 nests by phase (`nestCap`), so scarabs hint that a totem is close. No totem ever has more than 2 nests (`nestsPerTarget`), so the total shrinks as totems fall and a last totem never gathers a swarm. In phase 3 the buried core gets nests the same way (one when it is buried, then a share like a totem), hinting where it lies; they go with it when it breaks or is replaced. Sand Swirl opens a nest and releases one scarab (`nestScarabs`) within that range. When a totem relocates, its unopened nests move with it. Released scarabs keep chasing from their current positions. Destroying the totem in order removes any unopened nest.

**Sand Scarab** (`sand_scarab`): a small, fast desert beetle (speed 2.6, 8 HP) that uses the ordinary alien chase and melee: it bites you for 4 or a placed Ruby for 3 at 1 tile, once a second. It is built like the Moon's mirror mite (a square body, two eyes, a hop) but as a plain beetle with no glow: a sand-gold rim, a light head band with two plain black eyes and two antennae, split bronze wing cases with a centre seam, spots and shine, and four little legs that scuttle. It swells a little as it bites.

## Dune Worm

**Dune Worm** (`dune_worm`): a regular Venus alien from phase 2 on (`wormFromPhase`; `wormChance` 35% of regular spawns, at most 5 alive; 9 HP, speed 2.8). It tunnels underground after you (never a placed Ruby), leaving a wide, faint trail of churned sand (a soft patch, a low ridge and a few loose grains) that fades over 2.5 seconds. Underground it cannot be damaged or pushed (charge attacks included) and is hidden from the minimap. Once it settles on a tile next to you (diagonals count) it rumbles for 0.2 seconds (`burrowRise`), then bursts up: if you are still beside it, it bites for 7 and knocks you back 2 tiles (`burrowKnockback`), then stuns you for half a second once you land (`burrowStun`). It then stays up and hittable for 1 second (`burrowUp`), sinks for 0.4 seconds (`burrowSink`, still hittable) and rests underground for 1 second (`burrowRest`) before it can surface again. A Sand Swirl whose reach covers one underground digs it up: it is dazed above ground for 3 seconds (`burrowDazed`) with no bite.

It is drawn as a moving sand mound while underground (shaking and spitting grit as it rumbles up) and, above ground, as a blocky sand worm rising from a dark hole and a ring of sand: banded segments, a dark head plate, two big pale eyes and a jagged mouth that opens as it bites.

## Sand Scorpion (phase 3)

**Sand Scorpion** (`sand_scorpion`): when phase 3 starts, five (`scorpions`) are buried on random open tiles away from you, the totems, relics and nests, and stay on those tiles for the whole phase (any other phase clears them). While fewer than five are alive, a new one is buried on another random tile every 35 seconds (`scorpRespawn`). Each has 40 HP and never moves, and nothing pushes it. Buried, it cannot be hit, shows only as a faint low mound (hidden from the minimap) and does not stop you healing Ruby nearby. When you stand beside it (diagonals count), it rises in 0.23 seconds (`scorpRise`) and stings: 7 damage and a fast 6-tile knockback (`scorpKnockback`, at 2.5× the usual knockback speed, `scorpKnockSpeed`). It stays up and hittable for 3 seconds (`scorpUp`), stinging again every 70 ticks while you stay beside it, sinks back in, and rests 1 second (`scorpRest`) before it can rise again. A Sand Swirl that covers it digs it up, dazed: it cannot sting for 1.5 seconds. The god panel's Reveal Totems toggle also shows buried scorpions (a faint ghost in a dashed bronze outline, and a bronze mark on the minimap).

It is built like the scarab: a square body with a light rim, a darker rust inner panel with segment lines, plain black eyes and four thin scuttling legs, with two square pincers that snap and a chain of small blocks curling up from its back to an amber stinger that jabs forward as it stings. It rises out of the sand and sinks back in, cut off at the ground line.

## Sand relics

Ancient relics lie buried in the sand from the moment the challenge begins, before the Sentinel arrives: five at the start, then one more every 12 seconds while fewer than nine are on the map, never more than three of one kind (`relicPerKind`) (`relicStart`, `relicGap`, `relicCap`), on random open floor away from you, the totems and each other, with a mix of kinds. A buried relic is off the map, cannot be hit and gives no hint: finding one is a lucky accident of where you swirl. Sand Swirl uncovers relics in its reach like totems (they rise out of the sand, show for 5 seconds and sink back in). One hit (`sand_relic`, 1 HP, a challenge object with no score) breaks it for its gift:

- Relics are built like the scarab and the other square bodies: a square with a light rim, a darker inner panel and a softly glowing icon with a pale core, bobbing slightly.
- **Healing Urn** (a jar-lipped terracotta square with a rose band and a pale cross): heals you and Ruby 20 (`urnHeal`).
- **Sun Disk** (a bronze square with four ray nubs and a gold core): halves Venus Veil's remaining cooldown (`sunVeilCut`).
- **Seer's Eye** (a stone square with a carved slot holding a cyan eye): for 2 seconds (`seerTicks`) shows where every buried totem lies, as dashed outlines in the world and slim cyan pillar icons on the minimap (never his buried core), drawn over everything, with every totem's sequence number over it in the world and on the minimap, both in the blocky pixel font (Press Start 2P) with a dark outline, gold for the next totem and cyan for the rest.

Breaking one shows a ring in its colour with sparks rising (no text).

## The Aegis Sentinel's attacks

- **Leap.** Every 14 seconds in every phase (`leapGap`, about 9.8 while shielded) he leaps from anywhere. He never starts a leap while his shield is broken: the countdown waits until it returns (a leap already under way still lands). A LEAP bar beside the shield bar fills gold as his next leap nears and turns red while he leaps. A gold target follows you for 110 ticks (`leapAim`), then locks red for 78 ticks (about 1.3 seconds, `leapLock`) while his shadow grows. While locked, a translucent red inner square grows steadily from the centre and fills the target exactly as he lands; its outline turns white once a Sand Swirl would parry the landing (`parryWindow`), so the counter is easy to time. After every landing, countered or not, he moves at no more than 1.8 for 1.5 seconds (`leapSlowSpeed`, `leapSlow`). He lands on the locked tile, dealing 18 damage within 1.6 tiles and shoving you 2 tiles (`leapDamage`, `leapRadius`, `leapShove`). While airborne he is out of sight and cannot be hit. The minimap shows the target.
- **Lance:** every 8 seconds in every phase (`lanceGap`) when you are within 10 tiles he stops and charges for 54 ticks (`lanceWindup`): a gold band aims at you, locks for the last 14 ticks (`lanceLock`) and turns white, then a beam 9 tiles long (`lanceRange`) hits for 16 (`lanceDamage`).
- **Softened hits:** his leap and lance hit you for only 80% (`attackSoften`: 14.4 and 12.8) but reflect at their full 18 and 16 (`reflectAs`), through the guard and Venus Veil alike.
- **More aggressive while shielded:** with his shield up, the time to his next leap or lance is 30% shorter (`shieldedPace`), and he moves at 2.6 (`shieldedSpeed`; 4 in endless), or about 1.04 while his shield is broken (`exposedSpeedMult`, 87% of the endless 1.19).
- **Lances more while broken:** with his shield broken, he lances every 1.8 seconds in every phase (`brokenLanceGap`, plus the charge), and a break cuts any longer wait down to that.
- Otherwise he hunts you or a placed Ruby with the normal Aegis Sentinel melee, which hits you for 7 here (`meleeDamage`; 12 in endless). He is still while leaping, charging or stunned.

## Celestial F

Venus Veil lasts 10 seconds, as elsewhere, but here it cuts 85% of the damage to you and Ruby (`veilDamage`; 65% elsewhere) and reflects direct attacks.

## HUD and look

- **Minimap:** totems are drawn last, over everything else including Ruby, as outlined cyan dots (amber while overloading) with a 14px number in Press Start 2P above the marker during their first appearance.
- **Floor:** staggered limestone paving follows the tile grid. [Venus map art](MAP-PREVIEWS.md).
- **Guardian bar:** AEGIS SENTINEL · PHASE n with three HP bands, then the shield: one section per totem of the sequence, emptying from right to left like the HP bands (destroyed ones dark, the next gold, the rest cyan), or a BROKEN bar counting down the exposure. The TOTEM badge sits to the right of both the phase title and the HP bar. It shows the number of the last totem destroyed in order. It clears when a fresh sequence begins.
- **Totems:** the Aegis totem model with no number; it rises out of the sand when it appears, is uncovered or moves, and sinks back in just before the sand takes it (0.4s each way, `sandTicks`, with a sand mound and grains spraying at its base), and shows as the totem mark on the minimap while visible. An overloading totem glows amber like an endless one.
- **Sand Swirl:** three turning amber ribbons sweep outward from the cast position, with grit caught between them. A thick border (a dark sandy underline under a bright line) outlines the fixed tile footprint at the cast location, and open floor inside turns subtly sandy: a light warm tint, faint wind ripples and a few grains, all fading with the sweep. A tight swirl follows the player while the guard lasts. The C icon shows three stepped amber wind bands narrowing into a funnel, with pale highlights and scattered sand grains. It dims and its rim fills while it recharges. [Sand Swirl icon](visual-reference/venus-sand-swirl-icon.png).
- **Leap:** the target square with corner brackets (gold while it tracks, red when locked) and a blocky shadow that grows as he falls; landing throws a gold shockwave and dust.
- **Lance:** a dashed gold aim band with a solid end line turns white as it locks. A solid amber charge fills the band from him outward with a white front edge and reaches the far end exactly as the lance fires, so the counter is easy to time. The fired laser has a soft outer band, a bright gold shaft, a narrow white core and an outlined tip. A parry turns the beam cyan.
- **Guard:** a sandy square around you while it is up.
- **Counter:** a white flash over the view, three diamond shockwaves (white, cyan, sand) racing out, a burst of cyan and sand shards, and a bright core where it was caught.
- **Sounds:** Sand Swirl plays `venus-sand-sweep.wav` at .9 through Other Game SFX. It is a short stereo wind rush with a dry grit tail. Each totem appearing or repositioning plays Saturn’s grant warning, `saturn-warning.mp3` at .9, normal pitch; a destroyed one .6, pitch 1.2, with the totem break; the shield breaking 1.4, pitch .9; its return .4, pitch .6. Overload warns with the urgent meteor beep and blows with `totem-explode.mp3` boosted to 1.8 (louder than in endless) over `sci-fi-explosion-09.mp3` at .6, pitch .7. The leap and lance wind up with `charging.wav`; the landing is `sci-fi-explosion-09.mp3` at .7, pitch .7, the lance `aegis-attack.mp3` at .8, pitch .8. A parry plays `aegis-attack.mp3` at .9, pitch 1.5, with `aegis-shield-break.wav` at .5, pitch 1.8.

## God panel

Phase 1–3 (fresh shield and sequence at that phase's HP), Break Shield (the same break as in play: heal, backlash and shatter effect), Swirl Ready, Leap Now, Lance Now, and a Reveal Totems toggle that shows every totem's position and sequence number, and every relic (a faint ghost in a dashed outline in its colour, and a coloured mark on the minimap) and scarab nest (a rust dashed box, and a rust mark on the minimap): totem numbers appear above their models and minimap icons, with gold for the next one. Buried totems, relics, nests and the core use their own silhouettes on the floor and minimap. The button uses the same cyan pillar icon as the minimap.
