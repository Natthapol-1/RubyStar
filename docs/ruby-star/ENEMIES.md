Movement is px/tick before the global ×1.02 [movement multiplier](GAMEPLAY.md#core-loop-map-and-controls). Standard direct damage is shown player / placed Ruby; special mechanics can use their own damage values.

The first time each late alien (Shooter, Enchanter, Bleeder, Snatcher, Impostor) appears, a NEW ALIEN callout shows its portrait, name and guide description at the bottom centre for 5s (`ALIEN_INTRO_TICKS`), with the constellation-choice chime at .35. Several new aliens at once queue one after another. On desktop it shows once ever (the save's encountered list); on the web, once per run.

Knockback from Armored, Runner, Ghost, Bomber and Lancer (`ALIEN_KNOCKBACK`) uses `shovePlayerFromEnemy`: a cardinal push away from the alien (the Bomber's blast centre), one tile at a time, stopping at walls. It is skipped while Lunar Echo takes the hit, during another push or Snowball roll, or after a fatal hit; Ruby is never pushed. While the player carries Ruby every push goes half as far (see [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md#core-loop-map-and-controls)). Any player knockback, from any source, gives one small screen shake when it starts moving the player (`KNOCKBACK_SHAKE_TICKS`/`KNOCKBACK_SHAKE_AMOUNT`, peak about 3px).

Most ordinary attackers choose player versus placed Ruby by proximity; carrying directs them toward the player. Healer/Shielder retain support AI; Leech explicitly ignores Ruby as a target. Charger damage occurs on actual tile arrival, not before contact. Its committed lane can be dodged sideways.

Leech deals no HP damage, does not drain shields or active powered time, and never creates energy from an empty gauge. Lunar Echo takes a decoy hit instead of losing real energy. God mode prevents theft. Meteor defeat returns only its stored energy, without standard kill rewards. Devourer absorption and debug removal are not defeats and give no refund. Storage is `Enemy.stolenEnergy`; display cells saturate at 60, but stored energy itself can exceed that.

Absorb shields take damage before HP; ordinary reward shields refill a minimum pool, rather than stacking. The [Saturn challenge](SATURN.md) uses additive shields and its own Snowbowl, Frosticle and Ice Cub enemies. Shielder's 50% reduction is separate from these pools and is applied before a Frost shield. Keep fractional damage; do not round low damage up to 1.

Non-boss unit HP bars share `enemy-health-art.ts` across Endless and all celestial maps, including custom aliens, plants, water clones and pylons. They appear above the unit only while injured, with the normal Endless dark-red backing, faint white border and green (>50%), yellow (>25%) or red remaining HP. Width and headroom follow the configured body size. Guardian copies retain their guardian disguise; ordinary alien bars must not reveal the Moon Queen.

## Boss encounters

Ten boss encounter types. Astral Twins uses two linked bodies; the pair counts as one encounter, and a Devourer's descendants share one family encounter. First natural boss arrives after 50 seconds (46.5s countdown + 3.5s warning). After the last encounter is defeated, the next arrives after 35 seconds (31.5s countdown + 3.5s warning). Before stage 20, this timer freezes while a boss is alive. From stage 20 in Endless (including Ascension), another encounter can arrive 80 seconds after the last natural spawn (`ENDLESS_BOSS_OVERLAP_TICKS`), or 60 seconds from Stage 30 (`LATE_BOSS_OVERLAP_TICKS`), up to two active encounters. A due spawn waits for a free slot. On Windows, until the player's profile records a Fiery King defeat in Endless, the first natural boss of every Endless run is the Fiery King; after that the first boss is random like the rest (web runs, which keep no profile, are always random). Natural draws avoid any still-active boss type. Each shuffled pool uses all ten types before refilling and normally follows ten-stage blocks; active types are deferred if a block changes while an older encounter survives. Explicit God spawns do not consume that pool.

Spawn HP = `base HP + 15 * prior bosses defeated`; Twins split that stage bonus equally between their two bodies. Boss defeat restores player and Ruby HP fully, fills star energy and grants the appropriate celestial skill starting at the tenth defeat. Boss defeat rewards also apply to meteor kills. Removal must clean up that boss's curse/hazards; Queen death clears her echoes.

Encounter timers pause in menus. Eclipse stops these bosses’ movement/attacks and twin HP transfers; core exposure and revival timers continue during active gameplay. The gold/cyan Sentinel opens its armor when exposed; seal pylons crack with damage and glow amber during overload. Coral Dawn and blue Dusk have distinct melee/ranged poses, shared half-star emblems, and visible fall/revival effects. HP bars remain readable at game scale; a fallen twin's empty HP bar becomes its revival timer, a dim fill that drains to nothing; there are no overhead names, shield counts or pylon warning labels. God-panel summons initialize the complete encounter; No Mobs preserves constructs and both twins, while No Boss/replacement removes the whole encounter without rewards. After Ascension, Sentinel grants Venus Veil; Twins grant Binary Star.

Lunar Echo attracts all direct enemy targeting across the map, including bosses (except the Moon challenge Queen; see [Moon](MOON.md)). Attraction does not give unlimited attack range, chamber access or line of sight. Support/stationary bosses retain their behavior. Area hazards, real meteors and Prayer's Ruby ritual remain dangerous. AI temporarily substitutes the decoy target, then restores real player state in `finally`; never permanently move or hide the actual player/Ruby to implement decoy attraction.
