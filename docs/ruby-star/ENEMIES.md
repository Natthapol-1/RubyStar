In desktop Endless/Ascension, the first time each alien (except Normal, Mini Splitter and Queen Echo) or boss ever appears, a NEW ALIEN / NEW BOSS callout shows its portrait, name and guide description at the bottom centre for 5s (`ALIEN_INTRO_TICKS`), with the constellation-choice chime at .35. Several new aliens at once queue one after another. It uses the save's encountered list, so it shows once ever; the web version never shows it.

Knockback from Armored, Runner, Ghost, Bomber and Lancer (`ALIEN_KNOCKBACK`) uses `shovePlayerFromEnemy`: a cardinal push away from the alien (the Bomber's blast centre), one tile at a time, stopping at walls. It is skipped while Lunar Echo takes the hit, during another push or Snowball roll, or after a fatal hit; Ruby is never pushed. While the player carries Ruby every push goes half as far (see [Gameplay](GAMEPLAY.md#core-loop-map-and-controls)). Any player knockback, from any source, gives one small screen shake when it starts moving the player (`KNOCKBACK_SHAKE_TICKS`/`KNOCKBACK_SHAKE_AMOUNT`, peak about 3px).

Most ordinary attackers choose player versus placed Ruby by proximity; carrying directs them toward the player. Healer/Shielder retain support AI; Leech explicitly ignores Ruby as a target. Charger damage occurs on actual tile arrival, not before contact. Its committed lane can be dodged sideways.

Leech deals no HP damage, does not drain shields or active powered time, and never creates energy from an empty gauge. Lunar Echo takes a decoy hit instead of losing real energy. God mode prevents theft. Meteor defeat returns only its stored energy, without standard kill rewards. Devourer absorption and debug removal are not defeats and give no refund. Storage is `Enemy.stolenEnergy`; display cells saturate at 60, but stored energy itself can exceed that.

Absorb shields take damage before HP; kill reward shields (Armored, Shielder, Snatcher) add to the pool, up to 40 (`KILL_SHIELD_CAP`); a pool already larger from another source is kept. The [Saturn challenge](SATURN.md) has its own frost-shield economy and its own Frostbiter, Hailer and Glacier Hulk enemies. Shielder's 50% reduction is separate from these pools and is applied before a Frost shield. Keep fractional damage; do not round low damage up to 1.

Non-boss unit HP bars share `enemy-health-art.ts` across Endless and all celestial maps, including custom aliens, plants, water echoes and totems. They appear above the unit only while injured, with the normal Endless dark-red backing, faint white border and green (>50%), yellow (>25%) or red remaining HP. Width and headroom follow the configured body size. Guardian copies retain their guardian disguise; ordinary alien bars must not reveal the Moon Queen.

## Boss encounters

**Phases in challenges:** one hit never carries a challenge guardian's damage across a phase. On the Sun, Saturn, the Moon and Sirius, whose three phases are equal bands of one HP pool, a hit stops at the boundary and the next hit starts the next phase (`phasedBossStep` in `damageEnemy`). Jupiter uses unequal HP bands, capped at their current phase floor before immediately starting the next charge. Gaia holds each phase floor and starts the next phase on its next update, Neptune holds each floor until its own phase-ending mechanic, and Binary Star's twins lose one life per phase. `tests/phase-cap.test.cjs` checks it.

Ten boss encounter types. Astral Twins uses two linked bodies; the pair counts as one encounter, and a Devourer's descendants share one family encounter. First natural boss arrives after 50 seconds (46.5s countdown + 3.5s warning). After the last encounter is defeated, the next arrives after 35 seconds (31.5s countdown + 3.5s warning). Before stage 20, this timer freezes while a boss is alive. From stage 20 in Endless (including Ascension), another encounter can arrive 80 seconds after the last natural spawn (`ENDLESS_BOSS_OVERLAP_TICKS`), or 60 seconds from Stage 30 (`LATE_BOSS_OVERLAP_TICKS`), up to two active encounters. A due spawn waits for a free slot. On Windows, until the player's profile records a Fiery King defeat in Endless, the first natural boss of every Endless run is the Fiery King; after that the first boss is random like the rest (web runs, which keep no profile, are always random). Natural draws avoid any still-active boss type. Each shuffled pool uses all ten types before refilling and normally follows ten-stage blocks; active types are deferred if a block changes while an older encounter survives. Explicit God spawns do not consume that pool.

Spawn HP = `base HP + 15 * prior bosses defeated`; Twins split that stage bonus equally between their two bodies. Boss defeat restores player and Ruby HP fully, fills star energy and grants the appropriate celestial skill starting at the tenth defeat. Boss defeat rewards also apply to meteor kills. Removal must clean up that boss's curse/hazards; Queen death clears her echoes.

## Shared challenge alien pool

Sun, Gaia, Moon, Venus, Binary Star and Neptune share the normal opening: Normal at the start, Armored and Healer from 25 seconds, Runner and Charger from 45 seconds. After 60 seconds of active play, Ghost, Bomber and Lancer also join. Each common pool entry has weight four and Shooter one (`CHALLENGE_EXTRA_POOL`). From 60 seconds Normal loses its opening double weight and counts once like the rest (`normalWeight` 4; 33 in all): Normal, Armored, Runner, Healer, Charger, Ghost, Bomber and Lancer are about 12.1% of normal-pool selections each and Shooter about 3%. Before 60 seconds Normal keeps its double weight in the opening pool. These are weighted replacements within the usual spawn rate and population cap, after each map’s themed-enemy replacement rolls; themed enemy chances, phase rules and caps do not change.

Saturn and Sirius use separate spawning systems that include Normal aliens: Saturn’s baseline is 30% Normal (Glacier Hulk cap overflow also becomes Normal); Sirius’s baseline is 15% Normal before Penitent replacements. Their Normal slots draw from the same timed shared pool as the other maps (Armored and Healer from 25 seconds, Runner and Charger from 45, then Ghost, Bomber, Lancer and Shooter from 60), with the same weights. Their themed-enemy rolls, HP multipliers, timers and population caps are preserved. Jupiter and Singularity retain their own spawn systems. Endless and Ascension retain their stage-based pools.
